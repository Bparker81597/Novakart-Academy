# Dash Rocket Character Bible

## Official Status

This folder locks Dash Rocket's official NovaKart Academy character design.
`reference_sheet.png` is the source of truth for future assets, portraits,
NPC appearances, karts, animations, UI, and marketing materials.

## Core Identity

- Name: Dash Rocket
- Role: Adventure Racer
- World: Rocket Ridge
- Species: Rabbit
- Personality: Excited, adventurous, fearless, encouraging
- Ability: Trail Blazer
- Catchphrase: "Ready for blast off?"

## Visual Design Lock

- Primary silhouette: Energetic rabbit racer with tall ears, green eyes, big
  smile, raised adventure pose, goggles, and rocket/adventure gear.
- Primary colors: Green, yellow, white, warm tan, black, silver.
- Signature icon language: Rocket, star, trail, launch pad, explorer gear.
- Outfit: Green adventure suit, yellow star accents, belt, gloves, boots,
  goggles, compact jetpack/rocket gear.
- Kart direction: Green adventure kart with star and rocket details.
- Home: Dash's Launch Pad.

## Must Preserve

- Rabbit species, tall ears, green eyes, and upbeat expression.
- Green/yellow adventure palette.
- Goggles, rocket/star identity, and explorer gear.
- Playful, high-energy pose language.
- Launch pad and space-adventure motifs.

## Avoid

- Do not make Dash a generic bunny without rocket gear.
- Do not remove the goggles, star, or launch-pad identity.
- Do not make Dash reckless in a dangerous or scary way.
- Do not replace green/yellow as the dominant theme.

## Animation Notes

- Idle: springy bounce, ear wiggle, excited wave.
- Driving: playful lean, rocket boost anticipation.
- Celebration: star burst, blast-off jump, huge smile.
