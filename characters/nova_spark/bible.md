# Nova Spark Character Bible

## Official Status

This folder locks Nova Spark's official NovaKart Academy character design.
`reference_sheet.png` is the source of truth for future assets, portraits,
NPC appearances, karts, animations, UI, and marketing materials.

## Core Identity

- Name: Nova Spark
- Role: Young Inventor
- World: Inventor Heights
- Species: Human
- Personality: Creative, curious, optimistic, clever
- Ability: Rocket Boost
- Catchphrase: "I've got an idea!"

## Visual Design Lock

- Primary silhouette: Young inventor with large expressive eyes, purple hair in
  high puffs/pony shape, goggles/headset, tool pose, and compact kid-friendly
  proportions.
- Primary colors: Purple, violet, electric blue, silver, gold, white, pink.
- Signature icon language: Gear, light bulb, wrench, blueprint, robot companion.
- Outfit: Purple inventor suit, utility belt, gloves, boots, goggles/headset,
  tech accents and bright invention details.
- Kart direction: Purple invention kart with light bulb/rocket boost details.
- Home: Nova's Workshop.

## Must Preserve

- Human young inventor identity.
- Purple hair, goggles/headset, bright eyes, and cheerful STEM theme.
- Gear/light bulb/wrench/blueprint motifs.
- Purple/electric-blue palette with silver tech accents.
- Creative, smart, friendly expression.

## Avoid

- Do not change Nova into an animal mascot.
- Do not remove the inventor/STEM identity.
- Do not make the design overly realistic, adult, dark, or intimidating.
- Do not replace purple as the dominant visual theme.

## Animation Notes

- Idle: thoughtful bounce, tool twirl, excited idea gesture.
- Guidance: points to inventions, taps blueprint, nods.
- Celebration: light bulb spark, gear spin, joyful grin.
