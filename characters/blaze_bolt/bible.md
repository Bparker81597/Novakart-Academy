# Blaze Bolt Character Bible

## Official Status

This folder locks Blaze Bolt's official NovaKart Academy character design.
`reference_sheet.png` is the source of truth for future assets, portraits,
NPC appearances, karts, animations, UI, and marketing materials.

## Core Identity

- Name: Blaze Bolt
- Role: Speed Specialist
- World: Sunny Speedway
- Species: Tiger
- Personality: Confident, energetic, brave, encouraging
- Ability: Turbo Burst
- Catchphrase: "Let's light up the track!"

## Visual Design Lock

- Primary silhouette: Short heroic tiger racer with big friendly eyes, rounded
  kid-safe proportions, raised confident pose, striped tail, and racing goggles.
- Primary colors: Orange, red, gold, black, white, warm cream.
- Signature icon language: Lightning bolt, speed sparks, racing energy.
- Outfit: Red/orange racing jacket with lightning details, black pants, belt,
  gloves, red/black shoes, goggles on head.
- Kart direction: Orange/red speed kart with lightning markings and number 1.
- Home: Blaze's Garage.

## Must Preserve

- Tiger species and orange/black stripe pattern.
- Blue eyes, warm smile, and brave but friendly expression.
- Goggles, lightning chest emblem, and racing suit.
- Speed/sunny/garage theme.
- Bold orange/gold palette and high-energy pose language.

## Avoid

- Do not make Blaze scary, sharp, aggressive, or older teen/adult.
- Do not remove tiger stripes, goggles, or lightning identity.
- Do not shift Blaze into ocean, inventor, or space themes.
- Do not use muted/dark palettes as the primary look.

## Animation Notes

- Idle: confident bounce, small fist pump, tail swish.
- Driving: forward-leaning excitement, quick reaction poses.
- Celebration: bright lightning sparks, fist raised, big smile.
