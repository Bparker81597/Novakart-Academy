# Finn Tide Character Bible

## Official Status

This folder locks Finn Tide's official NovaKart Academy character design.
`reference_sheet.png` is the source of truth for future assets, portraits,
NPC appearances, karts, animations, UI, and marketing materials.

## Core Identity

- Name: Finn Tide
- Role: Ocean Explorer and Coral Coast Guide
- World: Coral Coast
- Species: Sea Otter
- Personality: Curious, kind, adventurous, helpful
- Ability: Wave Boost
- Catchphrase: "Adventure is everywhere!"

## Visual Design Lock

- Primary silhouette: Friendly sea otter explorer with round face, big blue
  eyes, pointing guide pose, compact child-friendly proportions, and goggles.
- Primary colors: Teal, aqua, ocean blue, white, warm browns, coral accents.
- Signature icon language: Anchor, waves, compass, coral badge.
- Outfit: Teal explorer outfit, white sleeves, utility straps, belt, boots,
  goggles on head, ocean-themed badges.
- Kart direction: Blue/teal water-themed kart with anchor/wave details.
- Home: Finn's Dock.

## Must Preserve

- Sea otter species and ocean guide identity.
- Goggles, teal explorer outfit, anchor/compass motifs.
- Kind, curious expression and helpful pointing gesture.
- Coral Coast water palette and nautical accessories.
- Brown fur, cream muzzle, and bright blue eyes.

## Avoid

- Do not make Finn a generic bear, dog, or fox.
- Do not remove the ocean/anchor/compass identity.
- Do not make Finn stern, scary, pirate-like, or villainous.
- Do not replace teal/blue as the dominant theme.

## Animation Notes

- Idle: gentle wave, curious look-around, light bob as if on water.
- Guidance: points toward objectives and nods encouragingly.
- Celebration: happy wave splash, compass sparkle, warm smile.
